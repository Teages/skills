# Interface: TextureBorders

**`Advanced`**

Stores the width of the non-scalable borders, for example when used with [NineSlicePlane](scene.NineSlicePlane.md) texture.

## Properties

### bottom

> **bottom**: `number`

bottom border in pixels

***

> **left**: `number`

### right

right border in pixels

***

### top
