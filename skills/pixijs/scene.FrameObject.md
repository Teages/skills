# Interface: FrameObject

**`Advanced`**

A reference to a frame in an [AnimatedSprite](scene.AnimatedSprite.md)

## Properties

### texture

> **texture**: [`Texture`](rendering.Texture.md)

The [Texture](rendering.Texture.md) of the frame.

***

### time

> **time**: `number`

The duration of the frame, in milliseconds.
